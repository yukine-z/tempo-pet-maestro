How does it know this is music?
  • It looks for a clear musical pitch, not just loud sound.
  • It checks whether the sound has a tonal spectrum instead of the flat, hissy pattern typical of white noise.
  • It waits for consistent musical evidence before the timer starts, so a single tap or random noise should not count.
  • When the music stops, it gives you about 5 seconds of breathing room before pausing the timer, so you can turn a page or move to another song without losing the flow.
  • The built-in metronome click is briefly ignored by the detector, so the metronome should not earn practice time by itself.
  • Audio is analysed locally in your browser. It is not uploaded or recorded.
