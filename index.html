How music detection works

The game looks for sustained pitched sound, clear frequency peaks, changing notes, and a piano-like tonal spectrum. Speech, claps, fans, and random bumps should usually fail the test. It deliberately waits for musical evidence before adding time.

No audio is uploaded or recorded. Analysis happens in your browser.
