How music detection works

This version is deliberately steadier. It looks for sustained pitch, strong tonal structure, and a non-flat spectrum. White noise is specifically rejected because its spectrum is much flatter than a piano tone. It also waits before switching music detection on or off, which reduces flicker.

No audio is uploaded or recorded. Analysis happens in your browser only.

Evolution: every 10 minutes of real practice advances your pet to the next illustrated growth stage. The stage is saved between days.
